Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026. Transparency regarding how ArabiccharacterCounter.com operates.

1. Overview and Core Privacy Model

At ArabiccharacterCounter.com, we are committed to safeguarding the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, how it is handled, and our foundational commitment to client-side text processing.

2. Your Text is Processed Locally in Your Browser

The primary utility provided by our website is the Urdu/Arabic Text Counter & Character Limit Checker. We want to be completely explicit:

  • Any text you type, paste, or analyze in our tool is processed exclusively on your device through client-side JavaScript.
  • Your text is never uploaded, transmitted, logged, or saved to our web servers or third-party servers by our tool.
  • When you close or refresh your browser tab, your entered text is immediately removed from browser memory.

3. Information Collected Automatically (Server Logs)

Like virtually all websites on the internet, our hosting infrastructure may collect standard, non-personally identifiable log information when you access pages. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Referring and exit pages.
  • Date and time of visit.
  • Operating system.

These server logs are used strictly for technical performance monitoring, server maintenance, security diagnostics, and DDoS protection.

4. Cookies and Advertising Practices

Our website may partner with third-party advertising vendors (such as Google AdSense) to display advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com) or by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites of interest. Please note that once you navigate away from ArabiccharacterCounter.com, we do not have control over that external website and are not responsible for the protection and privacy of any information you provide while visiting such sites.

6. Children's Privacy

ArabiccharacterCounter.com does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that our website holds personal information of a child, please contact us immediately.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements to our tool or changes in legal regulations. We encourage users to review this page periodically.
