About Arabictextcounter.online

Building dedicated, accurate, and private linguistic tools for the global Arabic and Urdu writing communities.

Our Mission

The Arabic and Urdu languages are spoken, read, and cherished by hundreds of millions of people worldwide. Yet, most text length counters and word count utilities on the web were built with only English and Latin alphabets in mind.

When writers attempt to count Arabic or Urdu sentences using generic counters, they encounter persistent issues: diacritics (Harakat / Tashkeel) inflate character counts unpredictably, punctuation marks like the Arabic question mark (؟) or Urdu full stop (۔) fail to register as sentence boundaries, and complex ligatures cause inaccurate word segmentation.

Arabictextcounter.online was created to solve these exact linguistic obstacles. Our platform provides a modern, fast, and completely free web utility designed from the ground up to respect the syntactic and typographic realities of Arabic script languages.

Who We Serve

Our utility serves a broad spectrum of communicators:

  • Urdu and Arabic Authors & Poets: Tracking word counts, chapter sizes, and poetic meters.
  • Academic Students & Faculty: Ensuring research papers, dissertations, and assignments strictly observe university length requirements.
  • Digital Marketers & Social Media Specialists: Tailoring captions, bio descriptions, and sponsored ads for platforms like X, Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.
  • Translators & Editors: Evaluating text expansion and contraction during translation workflows between English, Arabic, and Urdu.
  • Language Learners: Practicing composition while observing live feedback on word variety and sentence structure.

Privacy as a Foundational Principle

We strongly believe that a text counting utility should never act as a data collection tool. Writers frequently paste proprietary book drafts, sensitive legal documents, unpublished speeches, and intimate personal messages into online counters.

Because of this, all text analysis on Arabictextcounter.online is executed directly in your browser using client-side JavaScript. No text is ever uploaded to our servers, stored in databases, or reviewed by third parties.

Continuous Improvement

We are dedicated to refining our algorithms as Unicode standards evolve. If you have suggestions, encounter an unhandled character combination, or would like to provide feedback, please visit our Contact page.
