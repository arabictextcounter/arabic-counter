Frequently Asked Questions

Arabic, Urdu and English Text Counter

1. What is an Arabic Text Counter?

It is a free online tool that counts characters, words, sentences, paragraphs and lines in Arabic text.

2. Does this tool support Urdu?

Yes, our text counter supports Urdu, Arabic and English languages.

3. Can I count characters with spaces?

Yes, you can count characters both with spaces and without spaces.

4. Is this text counter free?

Yes, this online text counter is completely free to use.

5. Can I count words and sentences?

Yes, you can count words, sentences, paragraphs and lines instantly.

6. Do I need to install anything?

No, you can use this tool directly in your web browser.
